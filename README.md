# Duoidal linear species, factorization locality, and bar denominator products

Raeez Lorgat

The paper gives nonstrong duoidal structures on linear species and a full operadic definition of factorization locality. For positively root-graded Lie superalgebras with finite weight spaces and finite factorization, the algebraic bar gives a signed root product. Examples include the normalized Igusa cusp realization, positive Witt homology, and the Motzkin–Riordan relation. Comparing the factorization bar with the algebraic bar remains conditional on a compatible functor and the stated realization hypotheses.
